SOLMAR HEAT

An open-world crime game in Solmar City.
Steal cars. Outrun the law. Pull the Meridian Job.

Keyboard + mouse. All city, characters, story, art and sound are original works made for this game.

How to Play

W A S D / ArrowsMove on foot / drive
MouseLook around (click to lock the cursor)
ShiftRun
SpaceJump / handbrake in a car
EEnter / exit a car
Left clickShoot (on foot)
HHorn
MMute sound
Esc / PPause

Follow the yellow marker to your next objective. Stealing cars, hitting people and shooting raise your HEAT — the police will come for you. Lose them, or visit Lucky's Auto Paint (green $ on the map) to clear your wanted level for $150. Your progress saves automatically after every mission.
